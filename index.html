The Miners × Oatly · Fall/Winter 2026 · Seasonal campaign

Countryside Season

Autumn has been harvested.

Four drinks, four little stories, all set in a slightly surreal countryside world.

The brief was to launch the fall/winter seasonal collection (October–February) as one campaign across the whole network, funded by Oatly. I built the campaign from scratch: a fashion editorial on a working farm, where everyday rural chores are reimagined through each drink's ingredients, styling and a little absurdity. Serious faces, strange situations; never costume-y, never a farm advertisement.

The shoot fed every touchpoint: menus, posters, digital screens in cafés, paid social, the influencer program and barista-led tastings for creators in Vienna, Brno and Madrid. It also became a limited-edition printed lookbook that guests take home for free, pairing the editorial with step-by-step recipes for all six drinks of the collection.

My role
Concept, art direction and production lead: location scouting, casting, crew, props, styling and make-up moodboards, storyboards for each drink, on-set direction. Art-directed product photography and the lookbook.
Team

The Miners · Retail line redesign · 2025–2026

Affordable Premium

A packaging system for a brand moving up a tier.

The brand repositioned into affordable premium, and the retail line needed packaging to match.

I set the creative direction and wrote the brief: a minimalist system built on bold condensed typography, the brand's arrow, and colour accents coded by coffee origin. I directed and approved the designs across the whole line, from beans and drip bags to matcha and canned Nitro Iced Tea.

Two new products launched under the new system: coffee capsules, now sold in the e-shop and all locations, and a Christmas drip-coffee Discovery Set released for the 2025 holidays.

My role
Creative direction, design brief, art direction and approval across the line
Team
In-house designer: final artwork, 3D renders, product film
Line
Coffee beans · drip bags · capsules · matcha · Nitro Iced Tea · Christmas Discovery Set
The Miners · Spring 2026 · Seasonal campaign

Spring Is Served

The Last Supper, retold as a ritual of spring.

A modern reinterpretation of The Last Supper: not as religion, but as a ritual of taste, seasonality and togetherness.

A long communal table became the stage for four new drinks: Crème Brûlée Latte, Strawberry Matcha, Cheesy Peach Tea and Cheesy Raspberry Brew. Each flavour was translated into colour, texture and table styling, with floral compositions made together with a floral studio. Renaissance composition meets the language of modern fashion editorials.

The cast eats, drinks and shares the moment on camera, so the drinks read as part of a scene rather than as products on a counter.

My role
Concept and art direction: references, palette, styling and outfit direction, set design. Led production end to end, including casting and crew. Art-directed product photography.
Team
6 models, photographer, videographer, stylist, hair and make-up, floral studio
Rollout
All locations · menus, posters, in-café screens · paid social · influencer program
